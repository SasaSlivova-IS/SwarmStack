# Submit a completed issue

Load at submission only. Finish the whole required authorised outcome; do not stop early by listing unfinished work as a completed delivery. A genuine blocker gets a blocker report.

Send one compact packet: original problem/resulting behavior, exact base/candidate, requirement-to-observed-result evidence, resolved/rebutted findings and requested Root review/integration action. Link detail rather than paste logs.

Root review and required final user acceptance are subsequent steps, not already-earned proof. Keep the issue open until its agreed closure conditions pass. Child PASS does not close the parent.
